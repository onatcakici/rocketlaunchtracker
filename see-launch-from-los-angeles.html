Watching rocket launches from Los Angeles, CA

Vandenberg launches head south over the Pacific, which puts them in view of the whole LA basin. Evening twilight launches are spectacular — the exhaust plume catches sunlight at altitude and glows across half the sky (the 'space jellyfish' that floods social media). Look west-northwest.

Watching rocket launches from South Padre Island, TX

South Padre Island offers the classic public Starship view: ~10 km across the water from the pad, with Isla Blanca Park as the go-to spot. Expect a delayed, chest-thumping roar about 30 seconds after liftoff and crowds for every flight test. Distances are approximate.
